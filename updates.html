🚀 Launching Forest Trend Mapper v1.0.0

April 2026

I am thrilled to announce the first official release of the Forest Trend Mapper, an interactive geospatial web application powered by Google Earth Engine, built with Python using Solara and geemap.

This release serves as the official companion tool for our 2025 publication in Forests, allowing users to dynamically explore vegetation trends and the impact of climate and fire history on boreal forest vegetation dynamics in Interior Alaska.

✨ Key Features

  • Interactive Spatial Analysis: Draw a Region of Interest (ROI) directly on the map, set custom year ranges, and calculate vegetation trends (Kendall τ and Slope).
  • Comprehensive Data Integration: Access robust environmental data sources, including TerraClimate, MTBS (US fire perimeters), and AICC Alaska Fire History.
  • Time-Series Visualization: Click specific points to generate time-series plots tracking spectral indices (NDVI, NBR, NDMI) correlated with climate variables.
  • Data Export: Easily download point-specific time-series data as a CSV for offline analysis.

🌲 Potential Management Applications

  • Mapping Landscape-scale Trends: Designed to track forest change across Alaska to identify emerging disturbance hotspots.
  • Isolating Non-Fire Disturbances: Overlay historical fire perimeters to filter out burned areas, highlighting potential climate stress or insect activity.
  • Enhancing Aerial Surveys: Helps plan highly targeted routes before surveys, validate suspected damage post-survey, and expand total monitoring coverage.
